About Us

Welcome to Majna Inspired Perfume — where every scent is crafted with passion, care, and heart.

We believe that perfume is more than just a fragrance — it is a memory, a feeling, and a part of who you are. Each blend in our collection is inspired by beloved luxury scents, reimagined with our own touch: carefully selected ingredients, balanced notes, and 30% Oil Concentration designed to last and truly impress.

We don’t rush perfection. Every bottle is made with intention, attention to detail, and a simple goal — to give you beautiful, long-lasting fragrances that make you feel confident and radiant every single day.

We are a small business built on love for great scents and the joy of creating something meaningful. Thank you so much for choosing Majna, for trusting us, and for letting us be part of your journey.
